## About me

Hi, I'm Dmytro Hladkyi, macOS software developer. I work mostly on low-level macOS software with a focus on security, system behavior, and the edges where public APIs meet real product constraints.

My day-to-day work includes Endpoint Security, Network Extensions, Auth Plugins, Core Services, Swift, reverse engineering, research, and proof-of-concept development.

Before focusing on Apple platforms, I worked on smart home systems and electronics. I've gained a lot of interesting experience, and I hope to find the time to share it.


## What I’m going to write about

- Apple platforms, macOS internals, and system programming
- Endpoint Security, Network Extensions, reverse engineering, and security research
- Smart homes, home automation, electrical systems, and practical infrastructure
- Electronics, microcontrollers, firmware, and small hardware experiments
- Tools, workflows, architecture decisions, and engineering craft
